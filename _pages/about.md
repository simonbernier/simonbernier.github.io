Hi, welcome to my website. I teach physics at Jean-de-Brébeuf College in Montréal and I am currently transitioning into AI safety research. Before teaching, I completed an M.Sc. in quantum condensed matter physics at McGill University, where I simulated quantum many-body systems with tensor network methods.

Since July 2026, I have been supported by the Career Development and Transition Funding from Coefficient Giving to work on AI safety. In the last months, I have completed BlueDot's _Technical AI Safety_ course and the _Technical AI Safety Project_, worked through the ARENA curriculum and took part in the September 2026 cohort of the Iliad Intensive.

I write blog posts to share my previous and current research, and learn as I go. I also share project highlights along with their GitHub repos. This website is meant to be bilangual and I will translate my writing when I have the time.
